VulcaLap is one piece of aluminium with the shadow line of sawn timber. No core to fail, no laminate to lift — the look you drew, classified A2-s1,d0 and cut to your setting-out.

The plank, solved.

Timber boards weather, twist and burn. Composites carry cores that fail their own fire tests. VulcaLap starts from the metal instead: a solid aluminium extrusion, coated to read as timber from the street and specified like the engineered product it is.

  • 01
    One extrusionSolid extruded aluminium — no composite core, no laminate, nothing to delaminate.
  • 02
    Two profilesTG125 and T&G150 cover widths, with the 400 Series variant for wider layouts.
  • 03
    One addressDesigned, extruded and finished in Britain, on lead times measured in weeks.

The joint does the work.

Every course locks into the last. The tongue carries the fixing, the groove of the next plank conceals it, and the shadow line falls exactly where the drawing says it should.

  • Tongue-and-groove interlock — each course drops over the one below and closes the line against the weather.
  • Concealed fixing — driven through the tongue and hidden by the following course. No face fixings, no colour plugs.
  • Matching trims — corners, closers and reveals extruded and coated in the same finish.
